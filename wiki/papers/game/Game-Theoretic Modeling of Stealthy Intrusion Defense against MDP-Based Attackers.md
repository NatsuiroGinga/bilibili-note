# Game-Theoretic Modeling of Stealthy Intrusion Defense against MDP-Based Attackers

> 论文来源：CrossRef
> 识别文件：raw/papers/game/2603.09587_2026_Game-Theoretic_Modeling_of_Stealthy_Intrusion_Defense_Against_MDP-Based_Attackers.pdf

## 一句话

Effective proactive defense requires understanding not only where an attacker is but also how rationally they behave. Yet, in complex and dynamic environments, both types of information are highly uncertain. Most existing studies address location uncertainty but assume perfectly rational attackers, neglecting uncertainty in reasoning. The joint uncertainty of attackers&amp;apos; location and rationality introduces two key challenges: the Hierarchical Expectation Problem, arising from recursive belief inference, and the Unknown Cognitive Levels Problem, caused by limited knowledge of attackers&amp;apos; reasoning abilities. To overcome these challenges, we propose the Enhanced Partially Observable Stochastic Game (Enhanced POSG) framework, which integrates an Improved Cognitive Hierarchy (Improved-CH) model. The Improved-CH model extends the traditional cognitive hierarchy by eliminating the need for information on the attacker&amp;apos;s cognitive level or strategy constraints. An approximation algorithm is developed to efficiently compute strategies under this framework. Numerical experiments show that the Enhanced POSG model yields proactive defense strategies outperforming both experience-based and equilibrium-based strategies. Moreover, the results reveal key defensive insights: effective defenders tend to maintain concealment and prioritize control of peripheral areas where attackers are most likely to hide.
